Brama CLI command reference

brama subscription acquire¶

Buy one new account of a provider when every account in the pool has spent its plan and the pool holds fewer accounts than the operator allows. Weles creates the account on a fresh identity, pays for the plan the pool’s accounts hold with the purchase card in Skarbiec, writes its login row and subscription item to the vault and signs it in; Brama proves the new grant with a refresh. claude-code is the provider Weles buys.

Every brama maintain pass takes the same decision by itself, so an operator does not have to run this: when the pool is spent and below the cap the pass starts the purchase and reports acquisition: claude-code started (purchase_started). After an automatic purchase that failed or never reported how it ended, passes buy nothing until this command is run again, because each attempt can spend money.

Invocation¶

brama subscription acquire <PROVIDER> --reason <REASON> [--gateway <ORIGIN> | --gateway-consumer <NAME>] [--bearer-role <ROLE>] [--json]
InputRequired/defaultMeaning
<PROVIDER>requiredThe provider to buy an account of: claude-code.
--reason <REASON>requiredNonempty audit reason, journaled beside the verdict.
--gateway <ORIGIN>optional; this processRun the acquisition on the gateway that holds the vault (POST /v1/admin/subscription-pool/acquire); its console bearer is read from stdin.
--gateway-consumer <NAME>optionalResolve that gateway through Stado’s service directory as this consumer.
--bearer-role <ROLE>optionalRead the console bearer from the token field of the vault item that plays this role instead of stdin.
--jsonoptional; offPrint the verdict document instead of lines.

The decision¶

The cap is the operator’s own number, claude_code_accounts_max in src/subscription_dispatch/acquire/numeric-provenance.json with his words beside it; a provider with no declared cap is never bought. Accounts are the non-retired pool members counted by the account each declares in brama:account:; a member that declares none counts as an account of its own. Each member’s plan is read fresh from the provider’s own usage report (no quota is spent); a member is spent when a rate-limit block is in force or a current window is at its whole limit. The plan bought is the tier the provider’s profile names for the pool’s accounts (organization.rate_limit_tier, for example default_claude_max_20x).

Output and state¶

The first line is <provider> <result>: <detail>, then the accounts counted against the cap, each member’s standing (spent, available or unread), and the plan tier, subscription id, account and Weles run when there are any. Weles’s stages print on stderr while the purchase runs. result is acquired (exit 0), refused or failed (exit 1). Every purchase is journaled twice in BRAMA_STATE_DIR/journal.jsonl as subscription_acquisition: requested when Weles is asked, and the verdict when it ends. A new member claude-code-acquired-<UTC time> appears in brama subscription list; brama subscription hand-over signs it into a harness on the operator’s machine.

Refusals¶

Decided refusals carry a code: account_cap_reached (the pool holds the cap; the sentence names the cap and every account counted), pool_not_spent (names each account with plan left and its most spent window, or each account whose plan could not be read and why), pool_empty, plan_unstated (no grant stated its tier, or the pool holds more than one tier), account_cap_undeclared, inventory_unreadable, acquisition_running and, on a maintenance pass, previous_acquisition_unresolved. A failed purchase names Weles’s own code and stage, for example payment_declined, phone_verification_required, card_entry_failed or acquired_grant_unproven, and keeps the account, vault items and payment it already made. An unknown provider is refused with Weles buys claude-code accounts; `<provider>` is not one of them; the route answers it HTTP 400 and requires the brama-desktop console identity.
